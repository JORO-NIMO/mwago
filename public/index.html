Engineered for High-Stakes Presentations

Built from the ground up for zero latency, low hardware requirements, and offline reliability.

📱

Works on Android 4.0+

Strictly backward-compatible ES5 WebKit engine. Works on old Ice Cream Sandwich and KitKat devices from 2011 to the newest iPhones and Androids without downloading an app.

🎯

Smart Projector Lock

Never lose your mouse pointer on your laptop display again. With one tap, lock the cursor coordinates strictly inside the HDMI projector boundaries.

⚡

Sub-5ms Wi-Fi Latency

Zero cloud hops. Your phone connects straight to your PC over the local Wi-Fi or Ethernet router with intelligent delta buffering for butter-smooth tracking.

🖥️

Presentation Controller

Dedicated clicker bar for PowerPoint, Google Slides, and PDFs. Jump slides with PageUp/PageDown, toggle Fullscreen (F5), or exit with Esc.

⌨️

Wireless Text Beam

Need to search YouTube or type a URL on the big screen? Open the text dialog on your phone and beam typed text directly into the focused PC application.

🔒

Safe Spaces Sovereignty

Zero telemetry leaks, zero analytics tracking, and zero internet connection required. True local-first software following the Safe Spaces ethical AI manifesto.

Up & Running in 30 Seconds

No installer wizards, no drivers, and no administrative credentials required.

STEP 01

Run Mwago.exe

Download the standalone 7.9MB executable and double-click to run. It auto-detects all your connected monitors and starts your private local server.

STEP 02

Open Link on Phone

Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi. Open your browser and navigate to the address shown in the terminal (e.g. http://192.168.18.4:5000).

STEP 03

Control Your Screen

Slide your finger across the touchpad, tap to click, and use the presentation bar to command your projector from anywhere in the room.
